Businesses in ChatGPT is a new beta that lets a company claim how it appears in ChatGPT. In Business Hub, you verify the business, keep its details up to date and see aggregated numbers on how people engage with it. Claiming is free and doesn't need an Ads or Enterprise account. In the US, businesses can also set up an agent that talks to customers inside ChatGPT.
