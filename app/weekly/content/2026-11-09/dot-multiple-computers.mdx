---
title: One dot, several computers
source: OpenAI
date: 2026-11-09
category: ChatGPT
---
A dot can now connect to more than one of your local computers, and you choose which one it uses for a task. For example, it can build and test an app on a spare machine while you keep working on your laptop. Each computer needs the ChatGPT app and is approved separately. This is on top of the cloud computer every dot already has.
