source: Anthropic
date: 2026-11-10
category: Claude

Claude has three new kinds of artifact that you and your team can edit alongside it. Whiteboards lays a plan out on a board you can rearrange, and Sheets builds working spreadsheets with formulas, tabs and charts. Both are in beta on paid plans. Claude Data is the new name for Claude Dashboards, now generally available: live dashboards built from connected sources like a data warehouse or CRM. Anthropic also announced interactive connectors, which open partner apps such as Adobe and Atlassian inside Claude, next to the chat or full screen. All of it is rolling out gradually.
