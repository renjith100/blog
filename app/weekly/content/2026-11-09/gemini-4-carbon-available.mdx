source: Gemini

category: Gemini

Two weeks after showing it to a small group of cyber defenders, Google is rolling Gemini 4 Carbon out more widely. Google AI Ultra subscribers get it in the Gemini app, Antigravity and AI Mode in Search, and paying developers get it in the API. It costs $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output until the end of November, and twice that from December 1. Google still points to Argon for most coding and knowledge work, and to Carbon for the hardest, longest tasks.
