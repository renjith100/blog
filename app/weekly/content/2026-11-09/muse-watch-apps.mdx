---
title: Muse comes to Apple Watch and Wear OS
source: Meta
date: 2026-11-09
category: Muse
---
Meta's Muse agent now has watch apps. On Apple Watch you can type, dictate or start a voice call with Muse, read your main chat and open it from a complication on the watch face. A Wear OS version is out too, though Meta hasn't listed which watches it supports. Both need the Muse app on a paired phone.
