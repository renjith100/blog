title: Grok Bot can join your Tailscale network
source: xAI
date: 2026-11-10
category: Grok Bot

You can now add Grok Bot to a personal or company tailnet on Tailscale. That lets it reach private MCP servers, internal tools and apps without opening any of them to the public internet. It runs on Tailscale's OAuth apps, which became generally available the day before.
