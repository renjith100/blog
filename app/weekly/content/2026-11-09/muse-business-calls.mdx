title: Muse calls businesses for paid users
source: Meta
date: 2026-11-12
category: Muse
---
After a test that started in September, Muse can now phone US businesses for anyone on a paid plan in the US. It can book a table, make an appointment or wait on hold with an insurer. It calls from your own number, shows a live transcript and lets you take over. Calls are outbound and to businesses only.
