---
title: United stops AI agents from booking flights
source: Other
date: 2026-11-12
category: Agents
---
United Airlines changed its terms to stop personal AI agents from booking flights or managing reservations for their users. It cites cybersecurity risks and federal rules. The ban covers its website and app, and bookings that come through travel agencies and aggregators.

[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/united-airlines-blocks-ai-agents-from-booking-flights-citing-cybersecurity-risks/)
