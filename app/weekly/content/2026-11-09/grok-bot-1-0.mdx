title: Grok Bot 1.0 turns Bots into shared teammates
source: xAI
date: 2026-11-11
category: Grok Bot
---
Grok Bot is out of beta. The main change in 1.0 is that a Bot can be shared with a whole team and brought into Slack, Microsoft Teams and video calls on Google Meet, Zoom and Teams. Bots also spot work that needs doing and offer to take it on, and when a teammate teaches a Team Bot something new, the Bot holds it back until the owner or a Manager approves it. Team Bots are on Team and Enterprise plans, and meetings are on Team, Enterprise and Ultra.
