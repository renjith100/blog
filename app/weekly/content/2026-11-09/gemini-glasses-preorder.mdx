---
title: Gemini glasses open for pre-order from $395
source: Google
date: 2026-11-12
category: Gemini
---
Google and Samsung's Gemini glasses can now be pre-ordered, and they ship on December 10 in the US and South Korea. They have a camera, speakers and Gemini built in, but no display in the lens. Warby Parker's three styles start at $395, and Gentle Monster's six start at $449.
