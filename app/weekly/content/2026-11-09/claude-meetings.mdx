With Claude Meetings, you add Claude to a team call as a visible participant. Anyone can ask it something by voice or in chat, and it builds docs, whiteboards and other artifacts while the meeting is still going. Notes, action items and the transcript land on the meeting's page in Claude afterwards. It only works with Google Meet for now, and it won't join calls with outside guests unless an admin allows it. It's rolling out in beta on Team and Enterprise plans.
