With Claude Meetings, you add Claude to a team call as a visible participant. Anyone can ask it something by voice or in chat, and it builds docs, whiteboards and other artifacts while the meeting is still going. Notes, action items and the transcript land on the meeting's page in Claude afterwards. When outside guests are on the call, Claude doesn't reply in the Meet chat, and the guests don't get the meeting page. It only works with Google Meet for now. On Team plans it joins calls with outside guests unless an owner turns that off, and on Enterprise plans an owner has to allow it first. It's rolling out in beta on Team and Enterprise plans.
