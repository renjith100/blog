Hydrogen is the quick, cheaper member of the Gemini 4 family, meant for everyday work and agent workflows. It's rolling out in the Gemini app, Search, Gemini for business, AI Studio and the API, and to Pro and Ultra users in Antigravity. Free Gemini users get it when the app picks the model for them. The API price is $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output, with a 1M-token context window.
