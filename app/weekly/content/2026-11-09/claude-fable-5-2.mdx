Anthropic's new top models are one model under two names. Fable 5.2 is the public version, with safeguards that block high-risk cyber and biology work, and Mythos 5.2 goes only to organisations vetted through the Cyber and Life Sciences Verification Programs. Anthropic says they beat Opus 5.5 on almost everything it tested and write output up to 30% faster than the 5.1 versions. The API price drops to $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output, and Fable 5.2 blocks about 55% fewer requests than Fable 5.1 did. Fable is on all paid plans, but Pro users only get it inside their weekly limits until November 16. After that it runs on usage credits.
