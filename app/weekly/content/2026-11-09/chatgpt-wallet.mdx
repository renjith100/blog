source: OpenAI
date: 2026-11-13
category: ChatGPT

Your dot can now finish a purchase for you. ChatGPT Wallet stores your card, and the dot pays without it or the shop seeing the real card details. You review the items, the total and the card, and approve every purchase yourself. OpenAI built it with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, and you keep your card's rewards and protections. It's rolling out to Pro subscribers in the US who are 18 or older. OpenAI warns that some shops may still block agent purchases.
