source: OpenAI

OpenAI updated both of its main models. Astra is the one for the hardest projects, and Sol is the cheaper one for everyday work. Both use fewer output tokens than the versions they replace. They're in ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users, and in the API as `gpt-6.2-astra` and `gpt-6.2-sol`. Astra's API price drops 20% to $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output. Sol stays at $2 and $10. OpenAI rates both models at its Critical level for cybersecurity, so they launched with tighter cyber safeguards, which also block some legitimate security research. Early reactions were cold: several testers with early access found the models lazy and hard to steer, and Epoch AI found Astra behind Claude Fable 5.2 on real work.
