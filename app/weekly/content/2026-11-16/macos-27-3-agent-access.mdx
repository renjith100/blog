---
title: macOS 27.3 tightens what AI agents can reach
source: Apple
date: 2026-11-16
category: macOS
---
The second beta of macOS 27.3 splits Full Disk Access so you can give an app, such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, only the folders it needs instead of the whole Mac. Granting full access now takes a deliberate extra step and shows a warning. Apps that already have access keep it. The change is in beta for now and reaches everyone with the full 27.3 release.
