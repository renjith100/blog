Multiagent is a new effort level in Perplexity Computer, above Ultra. A lead agent running Claude Fable 5.2 plans the task and hands parts of it to up to 16 agents on models from Anthropic, OpenAI and Google. They work in parallel, share what they find and check each other's work. It's for Perplexity Max subscribers on the web.
