---
title: AI Studio builds full-stack Android apps
source: Google
date: 2026-11-17
category: AI Studio
---
The Build agent in Google AI Studio can now make Android apps with a real backend. When an app needs storage or sign-in, the agent sets up Firestore and Firebase Authentication itself. Gemini calls go through Firebase so the API key never sits inside the app. Apps can be published to your own Google Play library to test on your phone, and the in-browser emulator is faster and now plays sound.

[Google for Developers](https://developers.googleblog.com/build-full-stack-android-apps-with-gemini-in-google-ai-studio/)
