---
title: Gemini Tasks goes free, and you can text or email it
source: Gemini
date: 2026-11-17
category: Gemini
---
Gemini Tasks, the agent that used to be called Spark, is now free in the US, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia and India. Elsewhere it still needs Google AI Pro or Ultra. In the US you can also hand it work by text message, through Google Messages or iMessage, or by emailing gemini@google.com. It asks before big actions like sending messages or making bookings. Google notes the text channel isn't end-to-end encrypted.
