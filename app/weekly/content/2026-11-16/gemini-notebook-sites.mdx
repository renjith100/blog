---
title: Gemini Notebook turns your sources into websites
source: Google
date: 2026-11-16
category: Gemini Notebook
---
Gemini Notebook, the product formerly called NotebookLM, can now build an interactive website from a notebook's sources. There's no button for it. You ask in the chat, for example "make me a site", and describe what you want, like a study aid, a dashboard or a project tracker. The site opens in its own tab and can be shared. It's rolling out on the web to users aged 18 and over, including Workspace accounts, with the mobile apps to follow.
