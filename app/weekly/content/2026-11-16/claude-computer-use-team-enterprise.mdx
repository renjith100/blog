title: Claude computer use for Team and Enterprise
source: Anthropic
date: 2026-11-20

Claude can now see the screen and work in the desktop apps a user allows on Team and Enterprise plans. It's in beta and reaches organisations gradually. It works in the new Claude experience in Claude Desktop, and from Claude on the web and on mobile when "Computer access" is turned on.
