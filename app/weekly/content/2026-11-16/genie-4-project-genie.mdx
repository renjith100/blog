---
title: Genie 4 reaches Project Genie for Ultra subscribers
source: Google
date: 2026-11-18
category: Genie
---
Genie 4, the world model Google DeepMind previewed in October, is rolling out in Project Genie to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers. You describe a world, or start from a photo, and walk around inside it. Worlds now have sound, and you can change them while you explore. The new Project Genie adds an agent that helps build worlds and a gallery for sharing and remixing them. AI Pro subscribers can join a waitlist.
