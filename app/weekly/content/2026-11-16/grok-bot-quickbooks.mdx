title: Grok Bot drafts QuickBooks invoices
source: xAI
date: 2026-11-20
category: Grok Bot
---
Grok Bot can now connect to QuickBooks Online. It answers questions about cash flow and unpaid balances, and drafts invoices, estimates and payment reminders that you approve before they go out. The same update lets users choose, tool by tool, whether the Bot must ask first or can't use a tool at all. QuickBooks support is US only for now.
