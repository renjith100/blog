Neon is the new middle model in the Gemini 4 family. It's for coding, knowledge work and science, and it's cheaper and faster than Carbon, which Google keeps for the hardest long-running work. It's in the Gemini API and AI Studio as `gemini-4-neon`, in Gemini for business, and for Pro and Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app and Antigravity. The API price is $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output, with a 1M-token context window. Google says it ranks third on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index.
