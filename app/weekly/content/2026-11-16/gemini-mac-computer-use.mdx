---
title: Gemini for Mac can use your apps
source: Gemini
date: 2026-11-19
category: Gemini
---
The Gemini app for Mac can now click and type in desktop apps and websites to finish multi-step tasks. It works in a separate background window, so you keep your own screen and cursor, and you can watch, pause or stop it. It asks before it controls an app for the first time. It's rolling out in English in the US for paid personal accounts on Apple Silicon Macs. You turn it on in the app, then use Task mode and type "@Computer use".
