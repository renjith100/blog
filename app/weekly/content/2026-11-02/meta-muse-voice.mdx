---
title: Muse gets voice and a talking avatar
source: Meta
date: 2026-11-05
category: Muse
---
Meta's Muse agent now does realtime voice conversations, with an animated avatar whose face reacts as it talks and listens. You can interrupt it at any point, and it keeps working on tasks in the background while you talk. The avatar can sit next to the chat or go full screen, or you can use voice only. It's rolling out to adults in the US on mobile, web and Mac. AI glasses and the Muse Charm come later.
