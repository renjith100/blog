Personal Finance is a new beta in Claude. You link bank, card, loan and investment accounts through Plaid, and you can ask Claude about your balances and spending, compare months and catch things like duplicate charges. Claude can only read the accounts, so it can't move money. Anthropic says chats in the Personal Finance hub aren't used for training. It's for Pro, Max and Ultra users in the US.
