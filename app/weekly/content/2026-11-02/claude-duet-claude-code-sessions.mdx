title: Claude Duet controls your Claude Code sessions
source: Anthropic
date: 2026-11-06
category: Claude Code

Claude Duet, Anthropic's agent app, now connects to Claude Code. From your phone you can see what's running, approve changes and send follow-up instructions. It works with sessions on your own computer and in the cloud. Duet is still a US-only research preview, for people on Pro, Max and Ultra plans who have an invitation.
