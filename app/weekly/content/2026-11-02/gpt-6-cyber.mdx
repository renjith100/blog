GPT-6 Cyber is OpenAI's new model for security research. It's rolling out in the API and Codex to some approved Daybreak Red customers, and OpenAI plans to widen access over time. OpenAI rates it Critical for cybersecurity under its Preparedness Framework, one level above GPT-5.6 Cyber, and says teams at Mozilla, Palo Alto Networks and Cisco already use it.
