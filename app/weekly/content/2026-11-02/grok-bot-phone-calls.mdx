---
title: Grok Bot makes phone calls for you
source: xAI
date: 2026-11-04
category: Grok Bot
---
Grok Bot can now call businesses for you, for example to cancel a plan or book an appointment. It asks you to confirm before it dials and shows a live transcript during the call, and you can take over or hang up. Then it reports back in the chat. xAI hasn't said who can use it yet, whether it costs extra or which countries it works in.
