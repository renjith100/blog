Images 2.6 can turn a prompt or a picture into an animated GIF, and you can change the speed or edit single frames. You can also save reference images of yourself, pets or characters and use them again in later chats. It's in ChatGPT and Codex on every plan. Developers get two API models: Flare is built for speed, and Sunburst for quality and consistent edits.
