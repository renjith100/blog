---
title: ChatGPT and Codex will share one allowance
source: OpenAI
date: 2026-11-02
category: ChatGPT
---
OpenAI emailed subscribers that from December, Plus, Pro and Business plans will share one allowance between ChatGPT and Codex, measured in tokens. Work and Codex already share one. What's new is that Thinking chats in ChatGPT will join it, so heavy Codex use leaves less for them. Quick Instant replies still won't count. OpenAI also plans to merge the Chat and Work tabs, starting with a small test group. It hasn't said how big the pool will be.
