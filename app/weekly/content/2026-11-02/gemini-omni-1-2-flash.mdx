---
title: Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash makes 15-second videos
source: Gemini
date: 2026-11-05
category: Gemini
---
Google's new video model makes clips of 3 to 15 seconds, up from a 10-second maximum on Omni 1.1. Google says it keeps characters more consistent, gives better results when you edit a clip by asking for changes, and costs about 30% less, roughly $0.07 a second at 720p. It's generally available in the Gemini API and Gemini for business, and in Flow and Google Vids. In the Gemini app, 15-second videos are rolling out to Ultra subscribers.
