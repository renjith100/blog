Teammates are agents that keep working on a job without new prompts. They use your Computer tasks, skills, memory and tools, follow up on their own and can work with other Teammates. Each one has its own desktop and browser. You can add them to Slack so coworkers can give them work too. They're available now for Max subscribers on the web, and mobile and desktop apps are coming later.
