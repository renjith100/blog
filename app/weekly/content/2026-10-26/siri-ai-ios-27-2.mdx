---
title: Siri AI remembers your conversations
source: Apple
date: 2026-10-26
category: Siri
---
iOS 27.2 gives Siri AI memory of past conversations and more to draw on, like photos in Messages, calendar attendees, voicemails and Safari history. It adds French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish, and one setting now controls which apps Siri can reach. Siri AI is still a beta with a waitlist, on iPhone 15 Pro and later, and isn't available in the EU or mainland China.
