Grok Bot can now make Pages, shareable documents much like Claude artifacts. It also keeps logins and cards in its own encrypted Vault, which the Bot uses without ever seeing the secrets, or it can sign in through 1Password. On iPhone it works with Calendar, Reminders, Contacts and your location, and it makes better videos.
