Announced at GitHub Universe, Copilot Fusion routes each coding task across several models from Anthropic and OpenAI, at a Standard, High or Max effort level that you choose. GitHub says Fusion Max scores within two points of Claude Code on Opus 5.5 Max in Artificial Analysis's coding agent index, at around half the cost per task. It's on all Copilot plans in VS Code, the Copilot app, Copilot CLI and JetBrains IDEs.
