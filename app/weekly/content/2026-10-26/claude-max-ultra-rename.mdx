---
title: Max 20x becomes Ultra, with no 5-hour limit
source: Anthropic
date: 2026-10-30
category: Claude
---
Anthropic renamed its Claude plans: Max 5x is now just Max at $100 a month, and Max 20x is now Ultra at $200, with no change in price. Verified Ultra accounts no longer have the 5-hour session limit, which covers most existing subscribers. New Ultra accounts keep it until they're verified. Weekly limits stay the same.
