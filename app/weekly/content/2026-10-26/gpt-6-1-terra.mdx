OpenAI's new GPT-6.1 Terra is built for everyday professional work, with strong image understanding and precise computer use. OpenAI says it finishes tasks more than three times faster than GPT-6 Astra at about half the price of earlier Terra and Sol models, though it still points to Sol or Astra for the hardest coding work. It's in ChatGPT Work and Codex on paid plans, and in the API as `gpt-6.1-terra`.
