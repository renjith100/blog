Your ChatGPT dot, the personal agent in ChatGPT, now has its own phone number. Set up texting from the dot's profile and you can send it tasks, photos and voice notes from Messages, and it texts back with the same memory and connectors it has in the app. That includes kicking off Codex sessions and asking routines to report by text. It's in beta for Pro users in the US.
