---
title: Microsoft offers buyouts to a quarter of its staff
source: Microsoft
date: 2026-10-26
category: Work and agents
---
Microsoft offered a voluntary exit package to employees in the US, UK, Canada and India, which reports put at about 60,000 people. Microsoft frames it as putting the right talent in the right places. Coverage ties it to the company's push to do more of its work with AI agents. Employees have until early December to decide.
