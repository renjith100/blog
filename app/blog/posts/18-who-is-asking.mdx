The video is by **[One Timeless Witness](https://www.youtube.com/@OneTimelessWitness)**: [Consciousness is NOT What You Think: Ramana Maharshi's 3 Step Answer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nTLOOwjmZ4s). ## The short version

We look for consciousness as if it were something hidden in the body or the mind, or a feeling of peace we'll reach one day through enough effort. Ramana says that search is the confusion itself. So he starts with what consciousness is not.

1. **Not the body.** The body you had as a child is gone. Every cell has changed. But the one who watched that child's body and watches this one now hasn't changed.
2. **Not the mind.** Thoughts drift across like clouds and dissolve. Consciousness is the sky they drift through, and the sky doesn't come or go.
3. **Not an experience.** Joy, pain and even peace come and go. The one who knows them never does.

"Then am I nothing?" No. You are the presence that has never changed.

"So how do I find it?" You don't. You are it. The search itself is the obstacle.

What's left is to ask **"Who am I?"**, not as a mantra or a puzzle for the mind, but as a way of turning attention back to where it comes from. Every answer the seeker gives (the one sitting here, the one who thinks, the watcher) turns out to be something that can be named, and the I that can be named is not the real I. The body says "I am hungry", the mind says "I am thinking", the emotions say "I am angry". The one who knows all three is the I that can't be named.

You don't need to break free from anything. You were never bound. You believed you were the wave, but you were always the ocean.
